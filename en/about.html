About ClickZone

ClickZone is a site where you can play lightweight mini-games directly in your browser, with no install and no sign-up required. Our first game, Reflex Clicker, is a casual clicker that tests your reaction time in 60 seconds.

What we build

We aim to make games with clear, short goals, free of heavy tutorials or aggressive monetization. Each round should wrap up in 1-3 minutes, deliver instant feedback, and leave you wanting one more try.

The blog

Beyond games, we regularly publish articles about reflex training, focus, and light breaks. Browse all posts on our blog.
