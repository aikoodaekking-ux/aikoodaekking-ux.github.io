Reflex Clicker

Tap the circles that appear on screen for 60 seconds. Yellow circles give base points, green bonus circles are worth more, and red trap circles should be avoided. Keep your combo alive to raise your score multiplier.

AD SPACE

How to Play

  1. Press Start to begin a 60-second round.
  2. Tap or click the circles as fast as you can.
  3. Green bonus circles are worth more but disappear quickly.
  4. Red trap circles dock your score and break your combo if clicked.
  5. When the round ends, your score is recorded and your best score is saved automatically in your browser.
  6. Hit "Play Again" to start a fresh round instantly.
