Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

ClickZone ("the Site") takes your privacy seriously and complies with applicable regulations. This policy explains what information the Site collects and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

The Site has no account registration or login, and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name or email address. However, the following data may be generated or stored automatically:

  • Game records (local storage): Your best game score is stored only in your browser's localStorage and is never sent to a server. Clearing your browser data removes this record.
  • Access logs: Standard log data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited may be generated automatically by our hosting provider (e.g. GitHub Pages) and advertising networks.

2. Cookies and Advertising

The Site may display ads through third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense. Google and other vendors may use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's interests.

  • Google uses advertising cookies to serve ads based on a user's visits to this and other websites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • For more on third-party cookie usage, see Google's advertising policies.

3. Children's Privacy

The Site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). If we become aware that such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

4. Third-Party Sharing

The Site does not share user information with third parties except where required by law, or as needed for the operation of the advertising services described above, which may process data independently under their own policies.

5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in law or in the Site's services. Updates will be posted on this page.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please reach out via our contact page.
